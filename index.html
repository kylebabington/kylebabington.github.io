A closer look

How I build: FamilyFlow

The problem. Parents need a quiet window to cook, rest, or reset — and kids need something to do that fits the house right now: noise, mess, time, and whether an adult can help.

Who it’s for. Real households, not idealized ones. Parent mode sets hard constraints; kid mode asks for energy and style in plain language so children can steer without fighting the rules.

Decisions that mattered. Separate parent and kid modes so power and play don’t collide. Constraints as the source of truth before any AI suggestion. Timers and feedback so the activity lives in the room, not only in a chat reply.

Next. Richer profiles over time, better accessibility in kid mode, and suggestions that learn what actually worked for each family.

About

I’m a hands-on, visual learner who spends as much time outdoors as I do at a keyboard. Curiosity on the trail and in the workshop looks the same to me — notice what’s real, respect the people around you, and build something kinder than what was there before.
