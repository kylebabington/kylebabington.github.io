About me

Why my projects tend to look a little different from tutorial apps.

From operations to software

I came to software after years of hands-on work in the landscape and garden industry. That background taught me to notice operational friction, incomplete inventory, awkward customer workflows, and the difference between a feature that sounds good and one people can actually use.

How I work

I learn by building, testing, inspecting what breaks, and tightening the system. I care about clear communication, thoughtful data modeling, accessibility, and using AI as an engineering tool rather than magic dust.
